Nicks scampi

Ingredients

Shrimp + dry brine
- 1 lb medium shrimp, shell on (shells get saved for stock)
- 1 Tbsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda

Shrimp stock
- Shrimp shells (from above)
- 1 c dry-ass white wine
- 1/2 Tbsp peppercorns
- 1 bay leaf

Browning the shrimp
- 1 small shallot, finely diced
  (No shallot? Swap in ~1/4 of a small yellow onion, finely diced.
   Onion is sharper, so use a bit less and give it an extra minute to soften.)
- 1 or 2 serrano peppers
- 1 clove garlic, finely diced
- A little black pepper

Sauce
- 8 cloves garlic, sliced (not smashed)
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 squeezes of lemon
- Shrimp stock (from above)
- 6 Tbsp unsalted butter

Pasta + finish
- Pasta
- A few Tbsp pasta water
- 1 Tbsp marjoram
- 3 Tbsp parsley
- 1 Tbsp chopped tarragon


When to start what

- 0:00  Put a big pot of salted pasta water on to boil (leave it at a low
        simmer until you need it). Clean the shrimp and start the dry brine.
- 0:05  Start the shrimp stock while the shrimp brine. It reduces in about
        the same ~30 min, so both are ready together.
- 0:30  Stock is strained, shrimp are room temp. Prep shallot, serrano and
        garlic now if you haven't already.
- 0:30  Drop the pasta. Time it so it comes out just before al dente right
        as the sauce gets its shrimp back (check your box time; most pasta
        is ~8-10 min, and the browning + sauce steps take about that long).
- 0:30  Brown the shrimp, then build the sauce.
- 0:40ish  Pull the pasta and finish everything together.


Steps

1. Dry brine yo shrimp
   - Deshell and clean the shrimp. Put the shells aside for the stock.
   - Pat the shrimp dryish and toss with the salt and baking soda.
   - Set aside to come to room temp, ~30 min.

2. Make the shrimp stock (while the shrimp brine)
   - Combine shells, white wine, peppercorns and bay leaf in a small pot.
   - Bring to a simmer and reduce by half.
   - Strain, keeping the stock. Toss the shells.

3. Brown the shrimp
   - Add the shrimp, shallot (or onion), serrano, minced garlic and black
     pepper to a pan over medium heat.
   - Cook until the shrimp are slightly undercooked and it smells good.
   - Pull the shrimp out and set aside. Keep the pan.

4. Build the sauce
   - Into the same shrimpy pan: sliced garlic, red pepper flakes, lemon
     and shrimp stock.
   - Bring to medium heat at a light simmer, then add the butter.
   - Bring back to temp and toss the shrimp back in.

5. Pasta + finish
   - Pasta should already be going. Pull it just before al dente.
   - Toss pasta and a few Tbsp of pasta water into the pan with the
     undercooked shrimp. Shrimp and noodles should come together and be
     done at the same perfect moment.
   - Emulsify, then toss in the marjoram, parsley and tarragon.

Enjoy.
